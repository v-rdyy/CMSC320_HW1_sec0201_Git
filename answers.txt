Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git helps us keep track of changes, go back to older versions, test things on separate branches, and work with other people without overwriting each other's code.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be used for environmental monitoring. For example, satellite images and machine learning can help detect wildfires and identify the areas affected by them.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines two branches and keeps their original histories, while rebasing moves a branch's changes onto another branch to create a cleaner, more linear history.
